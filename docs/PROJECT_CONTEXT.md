# Eko Runners Club Website

## Project

Official website for Eko Runners Club, a Lagos running community.

ERC should not be positioned simply as a fitness club.

The brand is about:

- running
- community
- Lagos
- wellness
- friendships
- city culture
- training
- shared experiences

The site should become the digital home of the community.

---

## Primary Goals

1. Make people want to run with ERC.
2. Make upcoming runs easy to discover.
3. Communicate the culture of ERC.
4. Showcase the people behind the community.
5. Showcase events and photography.
6. Give brands a reason to partner with ERC.
7. Create a premium digital identity.
8. Eventually support dynamic events and community content.

---

## Main Audience

- New runners
- Experienced runners
- Lagos residents looking for community
- Existing ERC members
- People discovering ERC through social media
- Brands and potential partners
- Visitors attending ERC events

---

## Brand Positioning

Core statement:

WE RUN LAGOS.

Creative platform:

RUN THE CITY.

Supporting messaging:

Every pace. Every level. One community.

Come for the run. Stay for the community.

Running brought us together. Community keeps us showing up.

---

## Experience

The website should feel calm when stationary and energetic when moving.

Still state:
- editorial
- clean
- spacious
- premium

Scroll state:
- kinetic
- energetic
- fast
- alive

The final experience should feel more like an editorial sports publication or fashion campaign than a traditional fitness website.
