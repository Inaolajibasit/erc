## Never Do This

Do not add:

- glowing gradient blobs
- glass cards
- feature-icon grids
- generic testimonials carousel
- huge rounded cards everywhere
- blue/purple SaaS gradients
- random 3D spheres
- "Trusted by..." SaaS sections
- fake analytics
- fake community counts
- fake ERC partners
- fake member testimonials
- random stock photography
- excessive emojis
- AI-generated marketing copy without instruction
- use emojis 
- use unnecessary drop shadows
- dont use kicker texts or pre headers text 

Do not redesign sections merely because another implementation would be easier.

Protect the creative direction.
